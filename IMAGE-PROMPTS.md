# Frog portrait production

Tool: built-in image generation, with a targeted image edit. Final website asset: `frog-atlas.png`. No hand-drawn placeholder images are used for frog portraits.

## Generation specification

Create exactly 35 distinct realistic-but-slightly-weird frogs in a seamless 7-column, 5-row contact sheet with equal square cells, no gaps, borders, labels or text. Each frog is centered inside its own cell on moss, rock, leaf or reed, using natural-history macro photography, moist textured skin, natural anatomy, and soft colored backgrounds. Preserve row-major species order from `data.js` and `portraits`.

Colors in row-major order: fern green, apricot, speckled teal, lemon, copper, plum, mint; rust, pearl, black/gold, dusty rose, emerald, slate, chartreuse; cinnamon, lavender, turquoise, honey, scarlet, olive, frost blue; burgundy, jade, pumpkin, smoky grey, cream/green, lime, indigo/gold; coral, chocolate, pistachio, violet, brass, seafoam, white/black.

## Final edit prompt

Preserve exactly 7 columns by 5 rows, equal square cells, original species color identities and order. Dramatically vary body shape, size within frame, and orientation. Use slender tree frogs, broad flat frogs, spherical rain frogs, long-legged climbers, side profiles facing both ways, back-three-quarter views, overhead views, diagonal climbing poses and low crouches. Subjects range from 40% to 85% of their square. At most five direct symmetrical frontal views. Keep every frog inside its own cell. Realistic, slightly weird, no clothes, human poses or limbs crossing cell boundaries. Variation must be obvious at thumbnail size.
